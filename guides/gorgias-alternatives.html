Gorgias Alternatives: The Honest Comparison

Gorgias is the default choice for a lot of sellers — but "default" doesn't mean "best." Here's what the alternatives actually cost and deliver.

By Tianyuan Chen · Updated August 2026

The Problem With the Default Choice

Gorgias became the default Amazon helpdesk because it was early and its marketing is everywhere. But its pricing model has a structural flaw that most sellers discover only after their first big month:

Gorgias charges $0.90 per AI resolution. That sounds tiny until you realize that at 1,000 resolved messages per month, that's $900 in fees on top of your base plan. During Prime Day or Q4, your bill can triple — exactly when you can least afford it.

The Real Trade-Offs

Stick with a SaaS helpdesk if:

  • You need zero setup — sign up and go in an hour
  • You want pre-built integrations with Shopify, eBay, etc.
  • You have no technical comfort and no one to help

Switch to self-hosted AI if:

  • Your SaaS bill keeps creeping up with per-resolution fees
  • You care about your customer data staying on your server
  • You're doing enough volume that flat pricing saves real money
  • You want to own your support stack and never worry about vendor lock-in

What Self-Hosted AI Actually Looks Like

The self-hosted stack is open source and well-documented:

  1. Dify — the RAG platform that builds and queries your knowledge base (60,000+ GitHub stars)
  2. DeepSeek — the LLM that generates replies (GPT-4-level, ~1/20th the cost)
  3. Docker — deployment on your own server, one command

The result is a customer service agent that's functionally equivalent to a SaaS helpdesk's AI — but at a flat price, with your data on your server, and no usage-based surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is switching away from Gorgias painful?

The main pain is exporting your historical tickets and rebuilding your knowledge base. With self-hosted AI, you rebuild the knowledge base once from your product docs and policies. It's a one-time effort, after which you own everything and can't be held hostage by a pricing change.

Does self-hosted AI have the same features as Gorgias?

For customer service specifically — auto-reply, knowledge base, multi-language, escalation — yes. What self-hosted doesn't have is Gorgias's pre-built integrations and polished reporting dashboards. You trade some polish for cost, privacy, and ownership.

What's the break-even point for switching?

If you're spending more than $400/month on Gorgias (base + resolution fees), self-hosted AI is almost always cheaper. The one-time setup cost is recovered in 1-2 months, and from then on you're paying a flat $99-399 versus a variable, growing SaaS bill.
