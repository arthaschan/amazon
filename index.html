Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI customer service agent work for my Amazon store?

The AI agent is deployed on your own server using the open-source Dify RAG framework and DeepSeek LLM. It learns from your product documentation, past customer conversations, and returns policy. When a buyer sends a message, the AI retrieves the most relevant knowledge, generates an accurate response, and sends it — all in under 30 seconds, 24/7, in 12 languages.

What is the cost compared to hiring customer service agents?

Our Starter plan is $99/month plus a one-time $299 setup fee. A single outsourced customer service agent costs $800-1,500/month. Two agents to cover time zones? That doubles. Our Growth plan at $199/month replaces 2 full-time agents and delivers 24/7 coverage — saving over 90% in labor costs.

Where is my customer data stored? Who owns it?

All data is stored on YOUR server. Unlike SaaS solutions like Zendesk or Gorgias, nothing goes to our cloud. The code is open-source (Dify on GitHub with 60,000+ stars), so you or any developer can audit it. If you ever stop working with us, your system keeps running — there is no vendor lock-in.

Which languages does the AI agent support?

The agent supports 12 languages natively: English, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Chinese, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Korean, Arabic, and Russian. It auto-detects the customer's language from their message and responds in the same language with natural, human-like fluency.

How long does it take to set up?

From signing up to having your AI agent responding to customers, it takes 3-5 business days. We handle the full setup: server deployment (on your existing server or we recommend one), knowledge base construction from your product docs, and accuracy testing against real customer scenarios. You just provide the content.

Do I need technical knowledge to use this?

No. We handle all technical aspects — server deployment, configuration, optimization, and monitoring. You interact with the system through a simple English dashboard. If you do have a developer on your team, they get full access to the open-source codebase and can customize anything.

What happens during Amazon peak seasons (Prime Day, Black Friday)?

The AI agent scales automatically. Unlike SaaS solutions that charge per message or per resolution (meaning your bill doubles during peak), our fixed monthly pricing stays the same regardless of volume. The agent handles 50 or 500 concurrent chats with no performance degradation.

How do you ensure the AI gives accurate answers?

We use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), which means the AI only answers based on YOUR actual product data and policies — not generic knowledge that could be wrong. Every response is grounded in your knowledge base. Additionally, we run weekly accuracy audits and continuously optimize the knowledge base based on real conversation data.
